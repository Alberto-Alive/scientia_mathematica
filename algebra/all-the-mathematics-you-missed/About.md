📘 Thomas A. Garrity — All the Math You Missed (But Need to Know for Graduate School) 🧠✨

It’s a bridge / gap-filler 🌉 for advanced undergrads 🎓 and beginning grad students 📚 who feel like they “should know” a wide spread of core math before grad courses or qualifying/prelim exams ✅📝. The goal isn’t depth 🕳️❌ — it’s to give you the big ideas 💡, key results 🔑, and the overall “shape” 🗺️ of each subject, plus examples 👇, a few exercises 🧩, and pointers on where to learn it properly 📍📖.

The book is organized as a tour of many areas 🌍 (about 20 topics 🧭), with chapters like:

Linear Algebra 📐

ϵ–δ Real Analysis 🔍

Vector-valued calculus 🧮➡️

Point-set topology 🕸️

Stokes’ theorems / differential forms 🌊🧾

Curvature / geometry 📏🌀

Countability & axiom of choice ♾️🎲

Number theory (elementary/algebraic/analytic) 🔢

Complex analysis 🌀✨

Lebesgue integration ∫📊

Fourier analysis 🎶📈

Differential equations ⚙️📉

Combinatorics & probability 🎲🧠

Algorithms 🧑‍💻⚡

Category theory 🧩➡️🧩
